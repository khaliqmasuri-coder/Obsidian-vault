# 🟣 Ādāb Module

> [!info] Plug this in AFTER the [[Template - New Lesson|Core Lesson Template]] for Ādāb lessons.

> [!abstract] The Ādāb Difference
> In ʿAqīdah, you are measured by what you **believe**. In Fiqh, by what you **do**. In Ādāb, you are measured by what you have **become**. A lesson on tawāḍuʿ is not done until you are more humble — not until you can define it.
