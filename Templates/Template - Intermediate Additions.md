# 📚 Intermediate Additions — Khilāf Analysis

> [!danger] Most Important Addition at This Level
> Most khilāf is narrower than it appears. Students waste hours on the 80% where positions agree and miss the precise 20% where they diverge. Defining the **maḥall al-khilāf precisely** — before analyzing positions — prevents this waste.

---

## 🎯 Maḥall al-Khilāf — Precise Locus of the Dispute

| ✅ Both Sides Agree on This | ❌ They Disagree Specifically Here |
|---|---|
| *[List every point of agreement — prevents strawmanning]* | *[One precise sentence: the exact point where they part ways]* |

**Type:** Lafẓī (verbal only) / Maʿnawī (substantive)

## ⚖️ Tarjīḥ — The Stronger View

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| 🎯 **Rājiḥ** | *[Which position and why — stated precisely]* |
| **Evidence strength** | *[Whose evidence is textually stronger and why?]* |
| **Uṣūl support** | *[Which established qāʿidah supports this position?]* |
| **Salaf practice** | *[What did the Companions / Tābiʿūn do? — if available]* |
| **Main objection + answer** | *[State the best objection to the rājiḥ and how it is answered]* |
| **Confidence** | Strong / Moderate / Weak — *be intellectually honest* |
| **Khilāf strength** | Muʿtabar (respectable, both views valid) / Shādhdh (weak, should not be followed) |
