## ⚔️ Steelmanning — Required Before Tarjīḥ

> [!danger] Why Steelmanning Is Non-Negotiable
> Your tarjīḥ is only as strong as the version of the opposing view you are refuting. State each opposing position so accurately that its own proponents would say: *"Yes, that is exactly what we believe."*

> *[Write the opposing view so well that its proponents would nod in agreement. Include their strongest evidence and most compelling reasoning. Do not begin tarjīḥ until this is done.]*

## 🔍 Naẓar fī al-Adillah — Critical Evidence Analysis

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Sanad quality** | Ṣaḥīḥ / Ḥasan / Ḍaʿīf + specific reason — *for each position's primary evidence* |
| **Dalālah strength** | Naṣṣ / Ẓāhir / Mafhūm / Muḥtamal — *how strongly does the text indicate this ruling?* |
| **Applicability** | *General or specific? Abrogated? Restricted by context?* |
| **Jamʿ possible?** | *Can the evidences be reconciled without discarding either? If yes: do jamʿ first* |

---

## ⚖️ Qawāʿid al-Tarjīḥ — Principled Weighing

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Evidence strength** | *[Textually stronger — sanad + dalālah combined]* |
| **Uṣūl conformity** | *[Which position better aligns with established qawāʿid fiqhiyyah]* |
| **Salaf practice** | *[What did the Companions and Tābiʿūn consistently practice?]* |
| **Maqāṣid alignment** | *[Which serves preservation of religion / life / intellect / lineage / wealth?]* |

## 🎯 Taḥqīq al-Rājiḥ — Concluded View

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| 🎯 **Rājiḥ** | *[Stated precisely — no hedging, no ambiguity]* |
| **Reasoning chain** | *[Primary → Secondary → Supporting — three sentences]* |
| **Confidence** | High / Moderate / Low — *and the reason for that level* |
| **Khilāf status** | Muʿtabar (both views acceptable) / Shādhdh (should be abandoned) |
| **Practical fatwā** | *[Given the strength of khilāf, what is the practical stance for the layperson?]* |
