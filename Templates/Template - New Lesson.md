# 🕌 Pre-Lesson Protocol

> [!danger] Complete this before every lesson. It takes 2–3 minutes. Skipping it produces orphaned notes — information without a home.

---

## 🟢 Step 1 — Niyyah & Istiʿdād (Intention & Preparation)

> [!tip] Classical scholars wrote entire chapters on preparing the self before seeking knowledge. This is not optional formality — it is the condition that makes knowledge transfer to the heart.

| Field | Response |
|---|---|
| **My intention for this lesson** | |
| **State of my heart right now** | Distracted / Present / Tired / Alert |
| **What I am asking Allah for** | |
| **Istiʿdād (physical prep)** | Wuḍūʾ? ☐ · Facing qiblah? ☐ · Phone off? ☐ · Notebook ready? ☐ |

> [!tip] The Istiʿdād Gate
> If your heart is severely distracted, take 2 minutes of dhikr before beginning. Do not force knowledge into an absent heart. The scholars said: *"Knowledge is not mere narration — it is a light Allah places in the heart."*

---

## 📍 Step 2 — Mode Selector

> [!info] Circle before every session. Using the wrong mode wastes time or produces shallow understanding.

- [ ] 🟢 **First Study** — Complete all sections in order. Do not skip. Minimum 45–90 min.
- [ ] 🟡 **Rapid Review** — Speed Spine → Masāʾil → Taṭbīq only. Maximum 30 min. Do not open Tafṣīl.
- [ ] ⚫ **Deep Dive** — Full Tafṣīl + all Expand sections + source tracing. Research or teaching prep only.

---

## 🔴 Taṭbīq — Application

> [!danger] If you cannot answer these, the lesson is not done. Return to the Masāʾil and re-read. Do not proceed to the next lesson with incomplete Taṭbīq.

### 🎯 Scenario 1 — Error Detection

*Describe a real case where someone makes a mistake on this topic. → Which masāʾil principle refutes it? → State the correct position and its evidence.*

> [Your answer — write it or record yourself speaking it]

### 🎯 Scenario 2 — Distinction

*Give two cases that look similar but are different based on this lesson. → Map both using this lesson's framework.*

> [Your answer — write it or record yourself speaking it]

### 🎯 Scenario 3 — Teaching Test

*Explain the core concept to someone with zero background. → Use one analogy from daily life. → Do not use technical terms.*

> [Your answer — this is your Feynman Test]

### 🎯 Scenario 4 — Personal Application

*How does this lesson change something specific in YOUR life? Not in theory. Not in general. In YOUR salāh, your speech, your relationships, your intention. Name one concrete thing.*

> [Your answer — must be specific and personal]

## 🎚 Fahm Spectrum — Honest Self-Assessment

> [!info] Rate yourself honestly. ③ is the minimum before proceeding. Track progression over time. The date column is not optional.

| Level | Description | Behavioral Test | Date Reached | Current |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ① Heard it | Cannot explain without notes. Knowledge is inert. | Fails Taṭbīq Scenario 1 | | - [ ] |
| ② Get it | Can explain slowly with effort. Knowledge is fragile. | Passes Scenario 1, fails Scenario 3 | | - [ ] |
| ③ Can Teach | Can teach a beginner right now without notes. | Passes all 3 Taṭbīq scenarios without preparation | | - [ ] |
| ④ Own it | Applies spontaneously. Knowledge is internalized. | Uses the principle naturally in unrelated conversation | | - [ ] |

> [!danger] At ① or ②? DO NOT PROCEED.
> Re-read the Masāʾil. Redo Taṭbīq out loud. Sleep on it and retry tomorrow. Moving forward at ① or ② means you are building on sand.

## 🟡 Murājaʿah Schedule

- [ ] **Day 1** (today): Initial capture. Speed Spine written. Taṭbīq completed. Fahm rated.
- [ ] **Day 3**: Rapid Review — Speed Spine from memory only. If fails → re-study first.
- [ ] **Day 7**: Full Rapid Review + redo Taṭbīq Scenario 1 out loud.
- [ ] **Day 14**: Redo Taṭbīq Scenarios 2 & 3 out loud. Create/review Anki cards.
- [ ] **Day 30**: Monthly review. Re-rate Fahm. Has your level improved?
- [ ] **Pre-advancement**: All 5 Masāʾil from memory. Speed Spine without notes.

## 🔴 Ḥasb al-Nafs — Accountability Checkpoint

> [!danger] Before the next lesson, complete ONE of these. If you cannot — you took notes, you did not learn.

- [ ] **TEACH:** Explain this lesson to someone for 10 minutes (or record yourself) — then note where you went blank
- [ ] **WRITE:** Write a 1-page application to a real scenario you have encountered — without looking at notes first
- [ ] **ANKI:** Create 5 Tier-1 & Tier-2 Anki cards → pass each 3 times in a row
- [ ] **DEBATE:** Discuss this topic with a study partner — take the opposing position first
