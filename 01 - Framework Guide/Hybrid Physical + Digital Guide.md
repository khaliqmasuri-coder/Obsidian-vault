# 💻 Hybrid Physical + Digital Guide

You use both handwritten and digital. This page defines what lives where and when to transfer.

---

## 📝 What Goes on Paper

| Section | Why Paper Works Best |
|---|---|
| **Speed Spine** | Writing by hand embeds it in motor memory — significantly increases retention |
| **Masāʾil (5 bullets)** | Physical constraint enforces the 5-bullet limit — digital makes it easy to add a 6th |
| **Tasawwur** | Short enough for handwriting; drawing spatial maps is easier on paper |
| **Athar al-Qalb + duʿāʾ** | Heart content benefits from the slower pace of handwriting |
| **Matn lines (Beginner ḥifẓ)** | Repetitive handwriting is a memorization technique — do not type these |
| **Taṭbīq answers (rough)** | Free-form thinking works better on paper; transfer conclusions to digital |
| **Concept maps** | Drawing forces spatial organization — cannot be replicated digitally |
| **Fahm Spectrum ratings** | Quick pen tick on a printed chart; no need to open a laptop |

## 🔄 Sync Protocol — Paper → Digital Transfer (within 24 hours)

After every handwritten lesson, complete this checklist:

- [ ] Scan or photograph handwritten notes (name: `Subject_Lesson#_Date`)
- [ ] Transfer Speed Spine to Anki (Tier 1 gold card — highest priority)
- [ ] Transfer any Shubhāt to [[Master Shubhat Log|Master Shubhāt Log]] with SH-XXX code
- [ ] Transfer any Conflict flags to [[Conflict Register]] with CR-XXX code
- [ ] Update [[Master Learning Dashboard]] (lesson # and Fahm rating)
- [ ] Add Rawābiṭ cross-links in Obsidian (bidirectional `[[links]]`)
- [ ] Transfer Tafṣīl overflow into collapsible digital section

---

> [!caution] Backup Protocol
> Your notes are worth more than the books they came from. Back up digital notes weekly (cloud sync + local copy). Photograph handwritten notebooks when full. Do not store five years of knowledge in a single device.
