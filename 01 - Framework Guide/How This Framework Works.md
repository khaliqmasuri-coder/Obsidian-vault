# 🧠 How This Framework Downloads Knowledge Into Your Brain

> [!danger] The Problem With Ordinary Note-Taking
> Most students write notes and never look at them again. They mistake hearing with learning, writing with understanding, and coverage with mastery. This framework is built on brain science and the classical Islamic methodology of ʿilm to fix all three.

---

## The 5 Laws This Framework Enforces

### ① Retrieval Practice

You are forced to **recall** — not re-read. The Speed Spine, Taṭbīq scenarios, and Ḥasb al-Nafs all require you to produce knowledge from memory. Research shows retrieval is **3× more effective** than re-reading.

### ② Spaced Repetition

The [[Master Learning Dashboard|Murājaʿah Schedule]] (Day 1 → 3 → 7 → 30) mirrors the scientifically optimal spacing for long-term retention. Each review resets the forgetting curve.

### ③ Elaborative Interrogation

Every section asks **WHY**. Understanding the reason behind a ruling, attribute, or ruling cements it into existing knowledge networks — it is no longer an isolated fact.

### ④ Interleaving

Cross-Subject Integration forces your brain to switch between subjects, which feels harder but produces deeper, more flexible mastery.

### ⑤ The Feynman Gate

You do not pass any lesson until you can **teach it simply**. If you cannot explain it plainly, you do not know it — you are carrying a surface impression.
