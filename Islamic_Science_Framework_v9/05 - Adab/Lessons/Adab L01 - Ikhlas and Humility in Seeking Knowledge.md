# 🟣 Adab L01 — Ikhlas and Humility in Seeking Knowledge

## 🗺 Tasawwur
Adab protects the heart from turning sacred knowledge into ego-driven performance.

## 🔴 Masāʾil (5)
1. Foundation: Ikhlas is the life of knowledge.
2. Proof: Humility is a sign of accepted learning.
3. Application: Corrective self-audit is part of daily study.
4. Protection: Arrogance appears first in speech and debate posture.
5. Bridge: Integrates directly with Athar al-Qalb in all subject notes.

## 🟡 Speed Spine
- This lesson establishes that: the seeker is measured by inward sincerity and outward adab.
- The evidence is: revealed and transmitted praise of humility and intention.
- This matters because: adab sustains long-term benefit from ilm.
