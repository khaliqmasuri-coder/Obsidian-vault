# 🕌 Pre-Lesson Protocol

> [!danger] Complete this before every lesson. It takes 2–3 minutes. Skipping it produces orphaned notes — information without a home.

---

## 🟢 Step 1 — Niyyah & Istiʿdād (Intention & Preparation)

> [!tip] Classical scholars wrote entire chapters on preparing the self before seeking knowledge. This is not optional formality — it is the condition that makes knowledge transfer to the heart.

| Field | Response |
|---|---|
| **My intention for this lesson** | |
| **State of my heart right now** | Distracted / Present / Tired / Alert |
| **What I am asking Allah for** | |
| **Istiʿdād (physical prep)** | Wuḍūʾ? ☐ · Facing qiblah? ☐ · Phone off? ☐ · Notebook ready? ☐ |

> [!tip] The Istiʿdād Gate
> If your heart is severely distracted, take 2 minutes of dhikr before beginning. Do not force knowledge into an absent heart. The scholars said: *"Knowledge is not mere narration — it is a light Allah places in the heart."*

---

## 📍 Step 2 — Mode Selector

> [!info] Circle before every session. Using the wrong mode wastes time or produces shallow understanding.

- [ ] 🟢 **First Study** — Complete all sections in order. Do not skip. Minimum 45–90 min.
- [ ] 🟡 **Rapid Review** — Speed Spine → Masāʾil → Taṭbīq only. Maximum 30 min. Do not open Tafṣīl.
- [ ] ⚫ **Deep Dive** — Full Tafṣīl + all Expand sections + source tracing. Research or teaching prep only.

---

## 🔴 Step 3 — Muqaddimāt (Prerequisites)

> [!danger] Answer these before writing a single note. If any answer is "no" — stop and address it first. Building on shaky foundations is why students forget.

| **Key terms understood?** | |

> [!caution] If N on any above → STOP. Review the prerequisite. Return when ready. Do not proceed.
