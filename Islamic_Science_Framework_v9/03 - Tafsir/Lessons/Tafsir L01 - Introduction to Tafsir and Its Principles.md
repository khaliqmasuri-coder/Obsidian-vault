## 🗺 Tasawwur

> This lesson introduces the science of Tafsīr, its definition, importance, and the correct methodology for interpreting the Qurʾān according to the Salaf.

---

## 🔴 Masāʾil — The Spine (EXACTLY 5)

| Dot | Type | Masʾalah |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 | **Foundation** | Tafsīr is the science of understanding the speech of Allah — it is the key to every other Islamic science |
| 🟡 | **Proof** | "Do they not reflect upon the Qurʾān?" (al-Nisāʾ 4:82) — Allah commands tadabbur, which requires understanding |
| 🟢 | **Application** | The correct order of interpretation: Qurʾān explains Qurʾān → Sunnah explains Qurʾān → Companions' understanding → Arabic language |
| 🟠 | **Protection** | Interpreting the Qurʾān by personal opinion (raʾy) without following the methodology leads to deviation — "Whoever speaks about the Qurʾān without knowledge, let him take his seat in the Fire" |
| ⚫ | **Bridge** | Tafsīr connects directly to ʿAqīdah (verses on Allah's attributes), Fiqh (verses on rulings), and Ḥadīth (asbāb al-nuzūl) |

---

## 🟡 Speed Spine

> **This lesson establishes that:**
> Tafsīr follows a strict methodology — Qurʾān by Qurʾān, then Sunnah, then Companions, then language — and speaking without knowledge is forbidden.

> **The evidence is:**
> al-Nisāʾ 4:82 commands reflection on the Qurʾān, while the ḥadīth warns against interpreting it without proper knowledge.

> **This matters because:**
> Without correct tafsīr methodology, a person can make the Qurʾān say whatever they want — this is the root of every deviant sect's misuse of scripture.

## 🔴 Taṭbīq

### 🎯 Scenario 1

> Someone quotes a verse out of context to justify an unusual position. The correct approach is to check: what do other verses say on this topic? What did the Prophet ﷺ say? What did the Companions understand? Personal interpretation alone is rejected.

### 🎯 Scenario 2

> Case A: Interpreting "yad" (hand) in the Qurʾān as a literal human hand (tashbīh). Case B: Interpreting it as "power" metaphorically (taʾwīl). The Salaf's method is neither — affirm the attribute as befits Allah without likening or reinterpreting.

### 🎯 Scenario 3

> Tafsīr is like reading a letter from the most important person in your life. You wouldn't guess what they meant — you'd check if they explained it elsewhere, ask people who knew them, and then look at the language carefully. That's exactly how we approach Allah's Book.

### 🎯 Scenario 4

> I will start reading one page of tafsīr (Ibn Kathīr or al-Saʿdī) daily alongside my Qurʾān recitation, so I am not just reading but understanding.
