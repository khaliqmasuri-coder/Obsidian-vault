# 🟢 Tafsir L01 — Method and Limits of Interpretation

## 🗺 Tasawwur
The lesson establishes disciplined tafsir: text-first, narration-grounded, language-aware, and boundary-conscious.

## 🔴 Masāʾil (5)
1. Foundation: Tafsir begins with Qur'an by Qur'an and Qur'an by Sunnah.
2. Proof: Early generations are interpretive anchors.
3. Application: Context controls scope and prevents arbitrary extraction.
4. Protection: Personal opinion without method leads to distortion.
5. Bridge: Prepares for Asbab al-Nuzul and Munasabah analysis.

## 🟡 Speed Spine
- This lesson establishes that: tafsir requires method before conclusions.
- The evidence is: transmitted interpretive hierarchy from revelation and salaf.
- This matters because: structure protects from projecting assumptions onto the text.
