# 🔴 Aqidah L01 — Foundations of Tawhid and Intention

## 🗺 Tasawwur
This lesson anchors why every branch of Islamic knowledge is judged by correctness of creed and sincerity of intention.

## 🔴 Masāʾil (5)
1. **Foundation:** Tawhid is the axis for belief, worship, and interpretation.
2. **Proof:** Intention determines validity and acceptance of deeds.
3. **Application:** Correct creed protects from worship distortions.
4. **Protection:** Undefined intention opens the door to hidden shirk and showing off.
5. **Bridge:** Establishes method for all later subject study.

## 🟡 Speed Spine
- This lesson establishes that: sound tawhid and ikhlas are prerequisites for beneficial knowledge.
- The evidence is: "Actions are by intentions" and the command to worship Allah alone.
- This matters because: learning without corrected intention can become argument, not worship.
