## 🔴 Masāʾil — The Spine (EXACTLY 5)

| Dot | Type | Masʾalah |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 | **Foundation** | Tawḥīd is the right of Allah upon His servants and the purpose of creation — it is the single most important concept in Islam |
| 🟡 | **Proof** | "I did not create the jinn and mankind except to worship Me" (al-Dhāriyāt 51:56) — worship here means Tawḥīd |
| 🟢 | **Application** | Tawḥīd has three inseparable categories: Rubūbiyyah (Lordship), Ulūhiyyah (worship), and Asmāʾ wa Ṣifāt (Names and Attributes) |
| 🟠 | **Protection** | Affirming Rubūbiyyah alone is not sufficient — the Quraysh affirmed it yet were still mushrikūn because they violated Ulūhiyyah |
| ⚫ | **Bridge** | This connects to every subject: Fiqh rulings flow from Tawḥīd, Tafsīr explains it, Ḥadīth transmits it, and Ādāb is its fruit in character |

## 🟡 Speed Spine — Write LAST, Review FIRST

> **This lesson establishes that:**
> Tawḥīd is the foundation of the entire dīn, comprising three inseparable categories that define the Muslim's relationship with Allah.

> **The evidence is:**
> Sūrah al-Dhāriyāt 51:56 — Allah created all of creation for the sole purpose of worshipping Him alone.

> **This matters because:**
> Without understanding Tawḥīd properly, every act of worship risks being misdirected or corrupted.

---

## 🔵 Dalīl — Evidence Package

| Type | Citation |
|---|---|
| **Primary Qurʾān** | al-Dhāriyāt 51:56 — Purpose of creation is worship (= Tawḥīd) |
| **Primary Sunnah** | Ḥadīth of Muʿādh (Bukhārī/Muslim) — "The right of Allah upon His servants is that they worship Him alone" |
| **Supporting refs** | al-Ikhlāṣ 112:1-4, al-Fātiḥah 1:5 |
| **Salaf / Ijmāʿ** | Ibn ʿAbbās: "worship Me" means "single Me out in Tawḥīd" |

## 🔴 Taṭbīq — Application

### 🎯 Scenario 1 — Error Detection

> Someone says: "I believe Allah created everything, so I have Tawḥīd." This is insufficient — the Quraysh also affirmed Rubūbiyyah. Tawḥīd requires directing all worship exclusively to Allah (Ulūhiyyah). The dalīl is al-Zumar 39:3 where the mushrikūn admitted Allah was their Creator yet were still rebuked.

### 🎯 Scenario 2 — Distinction

> Case A: A person makes duʿāʾ to Allah alone at a grave. Case B: A person makes duʿāʾ to the person in the grave asking them to intercede. Both are at a grave, but Case A is Tawḥīd and Case B violates Tawḥīd al-Ulūhiyyah because duʿāʾ is worship and worship must be directed to Allah alone.

### 🎯 Scenario 3 — Teaching Test

> Imagine you have one boss at work. You do everything he asks. But then you also start taking orders from a random stranger and doing things for them too. Your real boss would say: "I hired you — why are you serving someone else?" Tawḥīd means Allah is the only Boss — you direct all your worship, love, fear, and hope to Him alone.

### 🎯 Scenario 4 — Personal Application

> I will audit my duʿāʾ this week to ensure I am asking Allah directly for every need, not relying on intermediaries or superstitions. Specifically, before sleeping I will make duʿāʾ only to Allah for my concerns.
