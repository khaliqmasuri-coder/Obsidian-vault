# 🔵 Fiqh L01 — Purification as the Gate to Worship

## 🗺 Tasawwur
Purification is not procedural detail; it is the structural gate for valid worship.

## 🔴 Masāʾil (5)
1. Foundation: Valid worship requires valid preconditions.
2. Proof: Purification is a shar'i condition for salah.
3. Application: Distinguish nullifiers from recommended acts.
4. Protection: Avoid treating preferred practice as legal obligation.
5. Bridge: Leads into detailed ahkam of wudu and tayammum.

## 🟡 Speed Spine
- This lesson establishes that: taharah is a condition that frames worship validity.
- The evidence is: revealed commands of purification before standing for prayer.
- This matters because: misunderstanding conditions corrupts worship outcomes.
