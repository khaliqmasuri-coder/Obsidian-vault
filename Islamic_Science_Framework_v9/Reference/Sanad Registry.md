# 📜 Sanad al-ʿIlm Registry — Chain of Knowledge

> [!danger] Why This Matters
> Record who taught you what. In the Islamic tradition, knowledge is not taken from books alone — it is transmitted through people. This registry is your isnād.
>
> When asked *"from whom did you take this knowledge?"* you must be able to answer. Ibn Sīrīn said: **"This knowledge is religion — look carefully from whom you take your religion."**
