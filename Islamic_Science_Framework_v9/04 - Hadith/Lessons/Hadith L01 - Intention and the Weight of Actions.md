# 🟡 Hadith L01 — Intention and the Weight of Actions

## 🗺 Tasawwur
This hadith is the interpretive key for sincerity, legal intent, and accountability in worship and conduct.

## 🔴 Masāʾil (5)
1. Foundation: Intent defines value and acceptance of deeds.
2. Proof: "Actions are by intentions" as an usuli anchor.
3. Application: Same outward act can differ in reward by intent.
4. Protection: Habitual action without intention erodes ihsan.
5. Bridge: Connects hadith sciences with fiqh and adab implementation.

## 🟡 Speed Spine
- This lesson establishes that: niyyah is a determinant of legal and spiritual weight.
- The evidence is: foundational narration on intention and outcome.
- This matters because: study, worship, and service are corrected by renewing intent.
