# 🟠 Conflict Register

> [!danger] Types of Apparent Conflict
> 1. **Textual** — two naṣṣs seem to oppose → attempt jamʿ first, then tarjīḥ
> 2. **Uṣūlī** — a ruling conflicts with a general principle → check if the principle has exceptions
> 3. **Personal** — new knowledge contradicts a position you held before studying → update your position, track the change

## Resolution Methods

| Method | When to Use |
|---|---|
| **Jamʿ (reconciliation)** | When both texts can be harmonized without discarding either — always try this first |
| **Tarjīḥ (preference)** | When one text is stronger in sanad or dalālah — only after jamʿ fails |
| **Naskh (abrogation)** | When the later text supersedes the earlier — requires clear evidence of chronology |
| **Tawaqquf (suspension)** | When you genuinely cannot resolve it yet — refer to a scholar |
